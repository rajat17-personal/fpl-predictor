<objective>
Open the model-class bracket D-03 mandates unconditionally: a stage-2 regressor swap seam in
`models/train.py`, three classical/GBDT candidates behind it, and the D-15 cheap gate that
decides which candidates earn a full 6-season walk-forward.

Purpose: LightGBM is the only regressor this project has ever fielded. Phase 9 measured that
added *inputs* do not move season points; the *model class* has never been varied. The value
here is closing that question with numbers, and the two-stage gate exists so closing it costs
three cheap validation fits rather than three 6-season retrains.

Output: a dev-only hash-locked lockfile behind a human package gate, `models/bracket/` with a
registry and three candidates, `train_position(..., stage2=...)`, and four recorded gate results
(LightGBM's own baseline plus the three challengers).

`GATE_MARGIN = 0.010` with a comment quoting D-15 and noting that MAE is recorded as a

`float(spearmanr(y_pred, y_true).statistic)`. Use `scipy.stats.spearmanr` to match
`backtest/benchmark_external.py::_stats_block` exactly rather than `_report`'s
`Series.corr(method="spearman")`; note in the docstring that the two agree numerically and that
the scipy form is chosen so every Phase 10 Spearman in the ledger comes from one call site.
Never define a fourth metric helper.

`run_gate(candidate, *, df=None) -> dict` — evaluate on the validation split only:
1. `load_features()` (or the passed frame), then `apply_experiment_feature_gating` with every

   absolute figure is therefore optimistic and is NOT comparable to the 0.383 pooled test
