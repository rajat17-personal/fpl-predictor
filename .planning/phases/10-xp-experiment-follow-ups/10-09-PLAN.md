<objective>
Build the one piece of infrastructure 10-RESEARCH.md flagged as its single most important
correction: there is **no** existing path for scoring an externally-produced prediction file
through the walk-forward harness. CONTEXT.md's canonical_refs describe an
`enrichment_preds_*` artifact-scoring path as an existing template; it was grepped and
confirmed absent.

Purpose: D-14 makes the local harness the sole judge of every Colab-trained bracket candidate,
and D-13 puts the two GPU-bound candidates on Colab. Without this seam, those candidates have
no adoption verdict at all. Building it now — before any Colab artifact exists — means no
verdict ever waits on infrastructure, and the seam gets tested against a known-good input
(a LightGBM run's own output) rather than against a candidate whose correctness is unknown.

Output: `backtest/walk_forward.py::load_external_predictions` with validation, an
implausibility check, and `tests/test_bracket.py` — proven by round-tripping a real
`_preds_for` output through the new path and getting the same `model+chips` back.
</objective>

`load_external_predictions(path, test_season, *, df=None) -> pd.DataFrame`:

   absent column at once.
2. Assert `set(artifact.season.unique()) == {test_season}`, raising `ValueError` naming both

   `test_season`, and inner-join the artifact onto it on `(season, player_code, fixture_id)`.
