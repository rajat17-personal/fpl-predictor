<objective>
Expand the tracer's single `av_chance_pct` column into the full availability encoding the
OpenFPL paper (arXiv:2508.09992) reports closed most of the ep_next ranking gap with only
categorical FPL availability tags, and make the D-10 safe fallback explicit across the whole
family rather than one column.

Purpose: 10-CONTEXT.md's Specific Ideas item is "mine OpenFPL's availability-feature encoding
before designing the P(play) features". The tracer proved the spine; this plan decides what
actually gets encoded, and adds the one signal the raw FPL fields do not carry — how stale the
resolved snapshot is, which is the difference between "this player is 75% likely to play" and
"this player was 75% likely to play nine days ago".

Output: `config.AVAILABILITY_COLS` grown from one to eight columns, the encoding implemented in
`data/availability.py`, registered in `CONTEXT_COLS`, and covered by staleness and
whole-family fallback tests.

The `availability_flags` gating branch, the `EXPERIMENTS` key, the resolver and the daily
capture all already exist from plan 10-01 and are not re-created here.
</objective>
