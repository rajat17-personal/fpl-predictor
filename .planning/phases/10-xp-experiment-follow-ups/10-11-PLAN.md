<objective>
Build the sequence representation D-20 specifies and the raw-torch training loop the deep
candidates share, then run the first deep candidate (the MLP) through the D-15 gate at both
D-18 granularities.

Purpose: the sequence-model hypothesis is explicitly "can a network learn better temporal
aggregation than our hand-rolled rolling windows" — which only means anything if the network
sees RAW per-gameweek history rather than the engineered `_r3`/`_r5`/`_r10`/`_rall` means. That
makes the sequence builder a brand-new leakage surface, which is why D-21 demands its own
explicit assertion on real data. Building the builder, its leakage gate and the shared training
loop against the simplest deep candidate first means plan 10-13's two GPU-bound candidates
plug into proven infrastructure.

Output: `models/bracket/sequence.py`, `models/bracket/deep.py`, D-21's leakage assertion, and
the MLP's gate result at the better of its two granularity variants.
</objective>
