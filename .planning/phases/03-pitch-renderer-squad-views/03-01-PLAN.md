<planner_corrections>
Two claims in `03-RESEARCH.md` / `03-PATTERNS.md` / `03-UI-SPEC.md` were re-verified against the
live exports during planning and are **wrong**. Trust this section over those documents:

1. **`squad.json`'s top level has five keys, not one.** Verified by reading
   `web/data/squad.json` during planning: `{ squad, captain, formation, cost, xi_xp }`.
   `03-RESEARCH.md` Pattern 3 and `03-PATTERNS.md` both describe it as `{ squad: SquadRow[] }`
   only, and `03-CONTEXT.md`'s data-contract note claims squad.json has "no formation field" —
   it does have one. Type all five fields. Derivation is still required (Task 1) because
   `/api/solve`'s `kind: "transfers"` response and `/api/rate`'s `xi[]` carry **no** formation
   field, so the pitch needs one uniform derivation for all three sources. The export's own
   `formation` value becomes a free correctness oracle for the derivation test.

2. **`chips.json`'s `dgw_clubs` / `bgw_clubs` are integer counts, not arrays of club names.**
   Verified from live data and from `predict/export.py:197-198`
   (`sum(1 for v in counts.values() if v > 1)` and `len(all_teams) - len(counts)`). This does not
   affect this plan; it is recorded here because plan 03-02 builds the chip timeline on it and
   the type lives in the `lib/api.ts` surface this plan writes.
</planner_corrections>
