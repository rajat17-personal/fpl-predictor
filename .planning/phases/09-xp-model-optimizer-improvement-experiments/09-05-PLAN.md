<objective>
Run D-01's experiment 4: fit Dixon-Coles / Poisson team attack and defence ratings on an expanding pre-gameweek window and feed them to the existing regressor as ordinary input features.

Purpose: the adopted odds features have two verified holes — the 2016-19 training seasons carry no odds at all, and future gameweeks in the multi-gameweek horizon have no posted odds, so the planner sees fixture difficulty but no calibrated strength signal for weeks t+2 onward. Ratings computed from results-to-date fill both, and unlike odds they update as teams change.

Output: `data/team_strength.py` producing one rating row per (season, gameweek, team); a guarded join in `data/build_table.py`; new feature columns declared in `config.py` and picked up by `features/engineer.py`; a decision-time-correct horizon graft; the `tests/test_leakage.py` assertion D-06 names explicitly; and an adoption verdict in IMPROVEMENTS.md.
</objective>

<measured_facts>
Verified against `data/processed/player_gw.parquet` on 2026-09-08, because this option's whole rationale depends on it:

- `team` (the club NAME column) is **0% populated for 2016-17, 2017-18, 2018-19 and 2019-20** and 100% from 2020-21 onward. That is why the odds join is null for those seasons, and why a name-keyed rating fit would inherit exactly the same hole.
- `opponent_team_id` is **100% populated for every season**, values 1-20; `team_h_score` and `team_a_score` are 100% populated (99.7% in 2019-20); `fixture_id` is 100% populated.
- For every season checked (2016-17, 2019-20, 2022-23) each of the 380 fixtures has exactly **two distinct `opponent_team_id` values and two distinct `was_home` values**.
- A row's own season-local team id is therefore reconstructible without the name column: within a `(season, fixture_id)` group the two distinct opponent ids are the two sides, and a row whose `opponent_team_id` is one of them belongs to the other. **Ratings must be keyed on the numeric `(season, team_id)` pair, not on the club name** — that is what makes the 2016-19 gap fillable, which is this option's stated reason to exist.
- Season-local ids are alphabetical per season and not stable across seasons. That is fine: every fit is within-season and expanding.
- `data/raw/<season>/merged_gw.csv` and `fixtures.csv` are cached for all eleven configured seasons, so a full `python -m data.build_table` rebuild needs no network access.
- A single-season retrain plus season simulation costs ~15 s (measured), so the pipeline rebuild plus a six-season A/B in this plan is minutes, not hours.
</measured_facts>
