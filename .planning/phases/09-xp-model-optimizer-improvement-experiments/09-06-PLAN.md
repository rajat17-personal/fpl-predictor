  <action>
Create `optimize/rl_env.py` defining `FplStrategyEnv`, a `gymnasium.Env` that steps one season one gameweek at a time.

Action space — strictly D-02's hybrid split. The policy chooses only **which chip to play this gameweek (or none)** and **how many transfers to make (0 up to a small cap covering free transfers plus a bounded number of hits)**. It must not choose players: `optimize/squad_ilp.py` and `optimize/transfers.py` remain the un-learned selection step, called from inside `step()` with the chosen chip mode and transfer cap. An action space that includes player identity reinvents the ILP badly and throws away its optimality guarantee; the acceptance criteria forbid it.

`action_masks()` must return a boolean vector excluding every illegal action at the current state, not merely penalising it: a chip already used in the current half, a chip already played this gameweek, a transfer count exceeding the free transfers plus the hit budget, and a wildcard or free hit in a gameweek where the squad cannot be legally rebuilt. Masking is the whole reason `sb3-contrib`'s MaskablePPO was chosen over plain PPO.

Reward — this is the single most important thing in the plan, and the reason FPL-RL's own headline was in-sample. `step()`'s reward is the **realised points for the gameweek net of transfer hits**, computed the way `backtest/season.py` computes it: the same autosub handling, the same captain and vice-captain fallback, the same `POINT_MULT[mode]` multiplier, the same `config.TRANSFER_HIT` subtraction. Reuse `backtest.season`'s helpers directly rather than reimplementing them, so the two can never drift. Do not use xP as the reward, not even as a dense shaping term — a dense xP proxy is exactly the optimistic-versus-honest trap this project has already walked into once with the +337-versus-+40 multi-gameweek finding.

Observation space — a compact float vector of decision-relevant state: current gameweek and its position within the half, free transfers available, bank, squad value, chips remaining as flags, and a small set of horizon xP aggregates computed with the same frozen-form construction `backtest/walk_forward.py::leakage_safe_plan` uses. No realised outcome may appear in the observation.

`reset(seed=...)` must accept and honour a seed so D-16's fixed-seed requirement is reachable; `step()` must return the five-tuple modern Gymnasium expects.

  - the action space is one-dimensional over (chip choice x transfer count) and its size does not scale with the number of players
