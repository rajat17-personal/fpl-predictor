<objective>
Port the fixtures ticker (`/fixtures`, UI-03) and the price watch page (`/prices`, UI-04) to
behavioural parity with the vanilla site.

Purpose: these two pages activate colour tokens Phase 1 declared but never used — the FDR 1–5 ramp
and the warn/destructive pair — and carry the phase's densest per-cell conditional logic outside
the xP table. The price page also carries the project's honesty posture: three different data modes
produce three different qualifying notes, and shipping the numbers without the right note would
overstate what the model knows.

Output: `components/FdrCell.tsx`, fully-ported `routes/Fixtures.tsx` and `routes/Prices.tsx`, and
four parity fixtures including one per watchlist mode.
</objective>
