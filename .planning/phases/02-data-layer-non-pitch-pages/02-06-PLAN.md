<objective>
Port the differentials page (`/differentials`) and the methodology page (`/methodology`) to
behavioural parity with the vanilla site, closing out UI-05 and the seven-page scope of this phase.

Purpose: differentials is the second consumer of the band cell and the status flag, and it is the
page where the deliberate divergence between the two `maxHi` formulas has to survive review rather
than be tidied away. Methodology is the only page in the phase whose content is bundled at build
time rather than fetched, and the only one that renders authored prose — which is why it is also
the only place a raw-HTML sink could plausibly appear.

Output: fully-ported `routes/Differentials.tsx` and `routes/Methodology.tsx`, and
`frontend/src/content/methodology.md` as a verbatim port of the vanilla prose.
</objective>
