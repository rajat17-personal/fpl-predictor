<objective>
Close UAT gap G-02-1: the dark theme reads green-tinted.

The four dark tokens that paint essentially the whole viewport (--color-bg, --color-surface,
--color-surface-2, --color-line) carry 1.2x to 2.3x the OKLCH chroma of their light-theme
counterparts while sitting only 4.9 to 12.7 degrees off the brand accent hue. Same hue plus more
chroma reads as a green wash rather than a dark surface with a green accent on it. Two dropped
vanilla-parity details amplify it: the React port declares color-scheme nowhere (vanilla declares
it at style.css:4 and :29) and leaves the body unpainted (vanilla paints it at style.css:55), so
light UA scrollbars and a white overscroll canvas sit against the tinted surfaces as a
true-neutral reference.

Purpose: make the dark frame read as dark. The tint was partly deliberate — every neutral in both
themes is green-family — so this rebalances rather than neutralizes: hue locks to the accent,
lightness is held exactly so the contrast ladder is untouched, and only the chroma excess is
removed.

Output: rebalanced dark neutrals in both the React rebuild and the vanilla site (changed in
lockstep, per the decision applied in Task 2 and recorded in Task 3), restored color-scheme and
body paint, a dependency-free OKLCH budget gate wired into the frontend test command, and the
spec and ledger records brought up to date.
</objective>
