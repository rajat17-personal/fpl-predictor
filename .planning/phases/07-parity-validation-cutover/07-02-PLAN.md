the full statement of the assumption is in `07-01-PLAN.md` under "Flagged Assumptions". It is
repeated here as a pointer, not re-resolved.

One further assumption is specific to this plan and is flagged rather than assumed silently:
**the eight-page field list authored in `extract.mjs` is a judgement call, not a derived set.**
CONTEXT.md D-05 names the categories ("table cell values, sort order, banner/copy text, key
figures") and leaves the per-page fields to Claude's discretion. If a field that matters is
omitted from the map, the diff will pass green while a real delta goes unseen — no `must_have`
can catch that. The mitigation is the manual checklist half of D-05, not the script.
